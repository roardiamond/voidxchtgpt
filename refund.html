Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Digital Products

Most products on VOID X HUB are digital (credits, tool access, tournament entries). Once access or credits have been delivered, refunds are generally not available.

2. When Refunds May Be Considered

Refund requests may be reviewed in cases of clear technical failure on our side that prevents delivery of the purchased item, or duplicate charges caused by a system error.

3. How to Request

Contact the Telegram support bot with your order / transaction details and a clear description of the issue. Requests are reviewed on a case-by-case basis.

4. Tournament Entries

Entry fees for tournaments/scrims are typically non-refundable once registration is confirmed, unless the event is cancelled by the organizers.
